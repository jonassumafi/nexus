Update flow, build spec

Final direction for the update prompt, based on the "one big number" idea. One centered card handles the update prompt, the download, the restart and the what is new list. Updates are required, so the card cannot be dismissed. The old separate recent fixes pop-up goes away. This page also covers the case where fixes ship without a new app version. Click through every state, try the three views, and read the rules table below.

State

View

Full shows the large card. Companion shows a smaller version. Radio-only shows a small title bar pill instead of a card.

Rules

CaseWhat happens
CheckingThe app checks at launch, about four seconds in, and then every 10 minutes while it runs. The check sends one small request and shows nothing when no newer version exists.
Update availableWhen a newer version is found, a centered card with a dark scrim covers the app. Title "Update available", line "Download to adopt the most recent features and fixes.", and one button, Download. There is no Later, no close button, and Escape does nothing. The window behind it is inert, so the update cannot be missed or skipped.
Full viewThe large card, centered over the whole window, required and with one button.
Companion viewA smaller version of the same card with the same wording and one button, centered over the compact window. Required, with no way to dismiss it.
Radio-only viewNo card, because the player is in game and the radio must stay clear. The update runs through a small pill in the title bar, beside the app name. (1) A round download arrow appears when an update is found. (2) Clicking it starts the download. The pill widens, fills from left to right as the download progresses and shows the percentage. (3) When the download finishes, the pill turns solid green and reads "Install and restart". (4) Clicking it installs and restarts, the same as in the other views. A failed download turns the pill red with "Retry". The after-update list and the fixes-only card do not show here and wait until the player switches to Companion or Full.
While transmittingProposed exception. If a push to talk key is held at the moment the card is due, wait until the key is released. This costs a few seconds and avoids cutting off a call.
DownloadingThe card shows the big percentage, size and time left. It cannot be hidden. If a newer version lands mid download, finish the current one first, then offer the newer one.
Ready to installA green check and one button, "Install and restart". A one line warning says the radio drops for about ten seconds.
InstallingAn indeterminate bar and one line of text. No buttons. The app restarts and the player stays signed in.
After an updateOn the first launch of the new version the same card shows "Updated to 1.4.2" with the what is new list. Continue closes it. This replaces the old recent fixes pop-up, which should be removed. It follows the same view rules, so Radio-only waits.
Fixes without an updateSome fixes reach players without a new app version, because the web side changed. When the changelog has entries the player has not seen, the same card shows once on the next launch. The eyebrow reads "Fixes since your last visit" and a note says no restart is needed. Got it closes it and records what was seen.
Skipped versionsA player who skipped several versions sees one list of the five newest entries across all of them, never a stack of cards.
Five entry capNever more than five entries. When more exist, a small "Older changes" link appears under the list and opens the full history on the website.
TagsEach entry has one tag. New for a feature, Better for an improvement, Fix for a repair. The tag list is fixed at those three.
First installNo card at all. A brand new player has nothing to catch up on, so the app records the current changelog id as seen and stays quiet.
Nothing newNo card, no sound. The check runs silently.
Download failsThe card shows a red mark, one plain sentence and a Retry button, with a small line of error detail to copy for bug reports. A failed periodic check while offline shows nothing, because the player did nothing wrong.
MotionThe card rises and fades in over about 280 milliseconds. All motion stops under the reduced motion setting.

Changelog data shape

The card needs a small list of entries, newest first. The app stores one value, the id of the newest entry the player has seen. Anything newer than that id counts as unseen, whether or not the app version changed.
