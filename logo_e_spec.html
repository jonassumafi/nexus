Nexus prompt mark, specification

Implementation spec for the new Nexus logo, built from option E: square brackets around a prompt chevron and a cursor. It ties the identity to the terminal look used across the app. The mark follows the player's skin, has a small size cut for favicons, and has a short boot animation. The live reference below is the source of truth, and the sections after it give exact geometry, color rules, files and checks.

0. Live reference

Pick a skin above and every mark in this page recolors from the same two tokens the app already uses, --blue and --cyan. The cursor blinks. Replay shows the boot draw-on.

1. Construction

Everything sits on a 64 by 64 grid. Strokes are 5 units with square caps and miter joins, so corners stay crisp when scaled. The grid below is drawn from the real geometry.

PartGeometryColor
Left bracketM20 10 H9 V54 H20, stroke 5ink
Right bracketM44 10 H55 V54 H44, stroke 5ink
Prompt chevronM24 22 L36 32 L24 42, stroke 5hot
CursorRectangle x 38, y 40, 10 wide, 5 tallhot
  • The brackets span 9 to 55 horizontally and 10 to 54 vertically, so the mark's visual box is 46 by 44 and centered on (32, 32).
  • The chevron and cursor share a baseline. The cursor's bottom edge (y 45) lines up within half a unit of the chevron's lower outer edge (about y 44.5), as a typed cursor sits after a prompt.
  • Do not redraw the mark. Use the source SVG in section 2 as is.

3. Color


Rule. The mark never uses its own palette. Brackets use --blue (the skin accent). The chevron and cursor use --cyan (the skin's bright accent). Both change with the skin, like every other piece of chrome.


ContextBracketsChevron and cursor


Dark surfaces (default)var(--blue)var(--cyan)


Light surfaces (docs, print, white pages)color-mix(in srgb, var(--blue) 52%, #000)color-mix(in srgb, var(--cyan) 46%, #000)


Mono (one color stencil, watermarks, tinted overlays)currentColor for all parts


Disabled or inactivevar(--t2) style muted colorSame muted color, no blink


The semantic colors (lime, red, amber) are never used in the logo, since they carry status meaning elsewhere in the app. The deep blue skin gives #66AADD and #00EEFF. Check the other eight skins in the switcher above, especially Obsidian and Dune where the two accents are close in value, and confirm the cyan parts still separate from the blue parts.

The subtitle text and the word "Nexus" come from the existing product naming. Confirm the subtitle wording before shipping and remove it from the lockup if it is not final.

AssetSpec


App iconRounded square, radius 22 percent of the side, fill --bg0, 1px border --rim1 (drawn at 1/128 of the side). The mark is the master geometry at 62 percent of the side, centered. Export 1024, 512, 192 and 180 pixel PNGs.


Maskable iconSquare with no radius and a full bleed --bg0 background. Mark at 52 percent of the side so the whole mark sits inside the 80 percent circular safe zone. Export 512 and 192.


FaviconSmall size cut, fixed default skin colors (deep blue values), transparent background. Ship as favicon.svg plus favicon.ico containing 16, 32 and 48 pixel images.


Electron iconThe 1024 pixel app icon as the source for the platform icons (.ico, .icns, .png), following the existing build config.


Optional: favicon follows the skin


When the player picks a skin, rewrite the favicon link with a data URI built from the small cut, filled with that skin's --blue and --cyan. Keep the static file as the default for first load and for browsers that ignore the change. This is a nice touch, not a requirement.

Inside terminals use the text glyph [>_] in --t-bright before the screen title, since the terminals are monospace text and the glyph is the mark's own shape. The blinking block cursor already in the terminals matches the mark's cursor, so it keeps its existing blink rate (1050 ms) and the logo cursor uses the same one.

7. Motion


EffectSpec


Cursor blinkOpacity 1 to 0, steps(1), 1050ms, infinite, 50 percent duty. The same rate as the terminal block cursor.


Boot draw-on (login, first load, boot sequence)Brackets draw over 360ms (stroke-dashoffset 1 to 0 with pathLength 1, ease-out). The chevron draws over 220ms starting at 280ms. The cursor appears at 520ms and starts blinking at 560ms. Total about 600ms.


Hover on a logo linkNo motion. Raise the cyan parts to full brightness only if the link is not already at it.


Static contexts (favicon, icons, emails, print)No animation, cursor shown solid.


Reduced motionCursor does not blink and stays visible. Boot draw-on is skipped and the mark shows complete.


Use the keyframes already defined in this page's CSS (blink, draw, curin) rather than rewriting them. Animate only the top bar mark on first load, never on every route change.





8. Do and don't


    

  • Do use the small cut under 24 px and the master at 24 px and above.
    • 

  • Do keep the brackets in the accent and the chevron and cursor in the bright accent.
    • 

  • Do let the mark follow the skin, and use the mono variant for one color uses.
    • 

  • Don't add outlines, glows, gradients, drop shadows or a containing circle. A rounded square is only for app icons.
    • 

  • Don't rotate, skew or stretch the mark, or change the order of the chevron and cursor.
    • 

  • Don't recolor parts with lime, red or amber, which are status colors.
    • 

  • Don't keep the old ring mark anywhere once the new one is in. Search for every use.
    •

  • The geometry matches section 1 exactly, with no extra effects.
    • 

  • Brackets use --blue and the chevron and cursor use --cyan, and both follow the skin in all nine skins.

  • Below 24 px the small cut is used and still reads as brackets, prompt and cursor in the tab bar on dark and light.

  • The cursor blinks at 1050 ms and stops under reduced motion. The boot draw-on plays once on first load.
    • 

  • The top bar, companion title bar and terminal titles use the placements in section 6 without changing bar heights.
    • 

  • Both lockups follow the ratios and clear space in section 4.
    •
