Activity feed, day groups and two lines

The chosen layout. Entries are grouped under a sticky date label, hang from a thin rail, and show a colored dot for type. Text is 11 pixels, clamped to two lines. Adjust the sliders to see how each variable behaves. Every value maps to a custom property on the feed element.

Rules

GroupingOne day label per calendar day, newest first, in the viewer's local time. Label format: Sun 1 Nov. Use Today and Yesterday when they apply.
Type dotsnew blue, log lime, join teal, done amber. Dot is vertically aligned to the first text line.
EmphasisPerson bold and bright. Action muted. Target tinted and semibold.
TimeLocal HH:MM, 24 hour. The date is carried by the day label.
OverflowEntries clamp with an ellipsis. Full text goes in the title attribute.
Hover7 percent teal wash over the row. No movement.
EmptySingle muted line: No activity yet.
