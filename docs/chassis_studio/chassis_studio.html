Design brief

What this is

Variation A (Chassis) with the blue taken out of the hardware. A theme is one block of variable values, so every surface, bezel, key, screw, LCD and the mock scene re-tint together. Nine themes ship, and Deep Blue is the original.

Semantics hold

Lime stays positive, red stays danger, amber stays ships and loans. Blue follows the theme accent, and the frequency LCD takes a tint chosen for contrast. Primary text is checked to WCAG AA in every theme, and the token panel below shows the live ratios.

Layouts

Companion has four ways to place five views: bottom key rail, left key rail, top rotary dial with card body, and a dashboard split. Full has four: console, sidebar, cockpit triptych, and a wide ops board with a permanent radio dock. Radio stays the 320 x 44 strip.

Combine freely

Mode, pin, dim, theme, layout and density are independent. Pick any theme and any layout, pin the window and turn the dim knob. Nothing is tied to anything else.

Theme variables

A theme sets these on :root[data-theme]. Everything else in the stylesheet reads from them or from values derived with color-mix, so a new theme is one block of about forty lines.

Nine themes

Values are read live from the stylesheet. The last three columns are computed in the browser: contrast of the three text levels on the screen surface (bg0), with AA for body text at 4.5 to 1.

Derived, not themed: --edge --hd-t --hd-b --cd-t --cd-b --tab-t --btn-t --av-t --inp --lcd-t --lcd-off --toast --trk-a --kt2 --kon-t --kc-t --mt0 --mt3 --led-off. Deep Blue overrides them with the exact Variation A values.
