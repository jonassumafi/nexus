Radio panel

The always-mounted WebRTC radio. In the Dashboard it sits at the bottom of the right rail and keeps its state while views change. It is the same component on the in-progress screen, where it auto-tunes to Mission.frequency. Everything on this page is interactive.

Live panelLive
Hold V to transmit. Peers speak at random so you can see the speaking indicator. Typing in a text field never triggers push to talk.

Behavior

  • Frequency range is 50.0 to 350.0 MHz in 0.1 steps. Out of range input shows an inline error and does not tune.
  • The push-to-talk key is read from User.voice_profile.ptt_key (default v) and shown in the header. Holding it transmits, releasing stops.
  • The LCD turns red and the button reads "On air" while transmitting. The speaker row shows a TX tag.
  • Peers on the frequency show an animated level bar when speaking. Mute silences incoming audio and zeroes the volume slider.
  • Collapse shrinks the panel to one row (frequency, TX, expand) so it stays out of the way on dense screens.
  • Leave drops the connection and returns to the untuned state.

Data sources

  • Frequency on launch: Mission.frequency
  • PTT key: User.voice_profile.ptt_key
  • Voice shaping (not shown here): voice_profile.highpass, lowpass, pitch_shift, bit_crush
