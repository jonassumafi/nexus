Design brief

Glasshouse treats the companion as light rather than hardware. Surfaces are frosted glass over a slow navy-to-teal mesh, edged with hairlines and tiny corner ticks, in the manner of a modern cockpit display. Information is drawn as well as written: a live waveform for the radio, rings for crew fill and rank progress, an arc for mission time. Controls are soft pills and sliding segments, which replace the bevelled buttons. Scaling is deliberate. Full is a three-pane glass layout with a floating radio orb. Companion becomes one column of cards with a bottom dock, so each task gets the whole width. Radio mode is a 44px luminous pill with a tiny waveform ring, and every other setting folds into its drawer. Text always sits on solid chips, so it stays readable when the pinned window is faded toward 30 percent.
