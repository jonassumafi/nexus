Design brief

Feel

A ship-grade handheld bolted into the cockpit. The calm digital screen sits recessed in a gunmetal-navy bezel with screws, vents, engraved labels, keys that travel 3px, a slide switch, status LEDs and a rotary dim knob.

Inspiration

Rugged field radios, avionics control heads and flight-deck handhelds. Personality moves out of the UI and into the hardware, so the screen stays quiet and readable.

Departure from today

The current build is a flat dark panel with a thin frame. This one reads as a physical object: material, bevels, noise, depth you can press. Colors and semantics are unchanged.

Scaling

Full is a console monitor with side vents and a bottom control bezel. Companion is a tablet with a five-key rail. Radio is a handset strip with a thumb PTT keycap whose drawer unfolds as a second panel. Mode, pin and dim stay physical controls at every size.
