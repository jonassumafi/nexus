    Design tokens introduced on this page

    Crew role colors are new. They tint the palette entries, the circular nodes on the schematic, and the role chips in review. The schematic surface is a dim grid with a faint hull outline, and nodes get the same raised treatment as buttons (hard 3px under-edge plus soft shadow).

    Pilot--role-pilot
    Turret--role-turret
    Engineer--role-engineer
    Medic--role-medic
    Marine--role-marine
    Navigator--role-navigator
    Schematic grid line--grid-line

    Schematic interaction. Drag a role from the list onto the diagram, or click a role to arm it and click the diagram to place. Drag a placed node to move it, click it to select, then remove it from the node list. Positions are stored as percentages 0 to 100 of the diagram width and height, which is what Ship.node_positions holds as x and y. crew_roles is derived by counting nodes per role.

    Field mapping. ship_name (uppercase), prefix (organization prefix), ship_model, description (max 1000), node_positions [{role, x, y}], crew_roles. Assumed rules: callsign 3 to 24 characters using letters, numbers, spaces and hyphens, callsign unique, at least one Pilot node, at most 12 nodes. Registration is blocked when the player already owns max_ships_per_player (2) ships.
