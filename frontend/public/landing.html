Why Nexus exists

Great moments take a crew, but most crews never form because nobody knows there's a mission.

Nexus keeps every mission on a shared board with its objectives, commander and open seats, so the right crew can find it and say yes.

Visible

Cooperative

Immersive

Immersion

It sounds like you are aboard.

Crew radio keeps everyone on your ship talking. Ship-to-ship radio keeps the whole fleet together. Each sits on its own frequency, so the chatter stays where it belongs and it feels like you are really aboard.

What it does

Everything a crew needs, nothing in the way.

Nexus pairs mission planning with a radio overlay on your desktop, so organizing and talking happen in the same place.

Two radio channels

Crew radio for the people aboard your ship and ship-to-ship radio for the whole operation, each on its own frequency.

26 radio profiles

A variety of radio styles and artifacts to choose from, so every player can customize how their own radio sounds.

Always-on-top overlay

Switch to radio-only mode to shrink it to the essentials and pin it above your game.

Mission and crew board

See who is flying what, which seats are open, and how full each ship is before launch.

Live activity feed

Newly posted missions, crew changes, and post-mission logs appear as they happen.

Friends and loaned ships

Add crew you fly with and borrow their ships for a mission, with their approval.

Make it yours

Nine skins, one click.

From the standard Deep Blue to Ember, Olive drab and Carbon pink. Pick one here to see the whole page change.
