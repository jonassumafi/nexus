Mission board specification

Hero ship cards under sticky status groups. Everything below is written so it can be implemented without guessing. The live example on the left uses the real markup patterns and is the visual source of truth. The ship image is a placeholder.

1. Structure

.board
  .board-head           "MISSION BOARD" + chip "N OPEN"
  .list                 scrolls, max-height follows the pane
    .group[.shut]       one per status, in order Waiting, Active, Completed
      button.group-head dot, LABEL, count, chevron (sticky, top 0)
      .group-body       animates height 0 to auto
        .card           one per mission
          .stripe       3 px, group color, full height at the left edge
          .hero         primary ship thumbnail, with fill underline
          .hero-label   the word Primary
          .rt
            .r1         title ........................ type tag
            .r2         ship name . posted ........... percent
            .r3         secondary ship strip ......... crew count

Open means Waiting plus Active. Completed missions are not counted in the chip. Empty groups are not rendered.

2. How the numbers are calculated

Percent on the card (right of the ship name) is the crew fill of the primary ship only: filled crew slots divided by total crew slots on that ship, rounded to a whole number. The count at the right of the thumbnail row (for example 3/22) shows the same two numbers.

Secondary ships show no numbers. Each one still gets its own fill rate, calculated the same way for that ship, and displayed as a thin colored underline beneath its thumbnail. The underline width equals that fill rate, and its color follows the same thresholds.

fill(ship)  = ship.total_slots === 0 ? null
            : ship.filled_slots / ship.total_slots      // 0 to 1

card.percent = round(fill(primary) * 100)               // text, right of ship name
card.count   = primary.filled_slots + "/" + primary.total_slots

underline.width = fill(ship) * 100 + "%"                // primary and secondary alike
underline.color = pct < 34  ? --red
                : pct < 67  ? --amber
                :           --lime                    // pct = round(fill * 100)

total_slots === 0  -> show "-" instead of a percent, no underline

The primary ship's underline sits under the hero thumbnail (3 px). Secondary underlines sit under each small thumbnail (2 px). There is no mission-wide percentage. The primary ship is the first ship attached to the mission, or the one flagged as primary if the data model has such a flag.

Check the data model: "filled slots" should be seats with an assigned crew member, and "total slots" the ship's crew capacity. Confirm these field names before wiring. Do not infer them from the screenshot.

4. Ship thumbnails

All thumbnails are neutral and monochrome (grayscale, brightness 1.35, contrast 1.05). Silhouettes identify the ship, so no tint is applied. The only colors near a thumbnail are the blue frame on the primary ship and the fill underlines. Each thumbnail carries a tooltip and an accessible label such as "Anvil Carrack, 5 of 6 crew".

Strip rule. The strip lists secondary ships only, because the primary ship is the hero. Thumbnails run left to right with a 6 px gap and never wrap. When the next thumbnail would pass the edge of the text column, hide from the end until a +N chip fits, where N is the number of hidden ships. Never hide the chip when N is above zero. Re-measure on resize and when a group opens. A mission with no secondary ships shows the muted words "Single ship".

7. Behavior

EventResult
New mission postedCard inserts at the top of Waiting. It fades and drops in 8 px over 280 ms with a brief lime wash that fades in 700 ms.
Status changesCard leaves its group (fade and slide 12 px right, 240 ms) and appears in the new group the same way.
Crew joins or leavesUnderline width and percent animate over 280 ms. Color changes at the thresholds.
Click a cardOpens the mission detail, as today.
KeyboardGroup headers are buttons with aria-expanded. Cards are focusable and open on Enter. Focus ring is 2 px gold.
Reduced motionAll durations collapse to near zero.
Empty boardOne muted line, "No open missions."

8. Copy rules

Titles keep the author's text and display in caps through CSS only. Posted time is relative for today ("just now", "18 min ago", "2 h ago", "yesterday") and a short date after that ("Sat 31 Oct"). The primary label always reads "Primary". Group labels read Waiting, Active and Completed.
