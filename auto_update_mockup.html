Nexus auto-update, live mockup

The companion app checks nexus-missions.space/api/releases/udp.yml about four seconds after launch. When a newer version exists it downloads silently and shows this small corner card to prompt the install. Use the scenario buttons to cycle every state the banner can reach. The reference grid below shows all five states side-by-side.
